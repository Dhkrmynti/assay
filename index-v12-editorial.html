05 · Directives

What NOVA

never does.

I.Never holds your keys.

Keys and seeds stay in your browser or your own wallet. NOVA only relays transactions you already signed.

II.Never lets AI touch money freely.

The AI decides inside limits written in code. Anything outside the limits is clamped.

III.Never makes up a number.

Every figure traces back to a transaction or a contract call anyone can check.
